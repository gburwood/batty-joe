HOW TO PLAY

MOVE

Use ← / → or A / D. The paddle has a little inertia, because apparently even paddles need character development.

LAUNCH / FIRE

Space launches a held ball, releases Sticky, fires Laser, fires the Invaders ship, and fires the FPS rifle. While a ball is held, slide the bat underneath it: release near the centre for a near-vertical launch or nearer an edge to aim toward that side.

BRICKS

Colours, symbols and damage marks reveal brick properties. Grey diamond bricks are indestructible during ordinary play.

POWER-UPS

Catch falling capsules. Some stack, some replace incompatible effects, and some are spectacularly unhelpful.

FRENZY

Rare Frenzy capsules temporarily turn Batty Joe into another arcade game: Invaders, FPS, Pinball, Asteroids, Missile Command, Arkanoid Revenge, 10-Pin or Bomber. In FPS Frenzy, watch for telegraphed bricks dropping vertically from above and strafe with the arrow keys. Each mode preserves the surviving brick state when normal play returns. A level can normally contain up to two Frenzies.

SPIN & FINAL ASSAULT

Move the bat as you strike the ball to impart spin and curve its flight. During Final Assault the ball accelerates and the remaining bricks exert increasingly obvious magnetic pull. BIG BOMB power is separate: special BIG BOMB POWER pickups appear independently during a level. Collect enough segments to arm the bomb, then press Down Arrow to deal two damage to every remaining destructible brick.

PAUSE

Escape pauses. F toggles fullscreen. Three lives, up to three continues, twenty levels, one very large final brick.
